Gross Domestic Product, or GDP, is the total market value of all final goods and services produced inside a country in a given period, usually a quarter or a year.

"Final" means we count only the finished product. The wheat a baker buys is not counted separately from the bread, otherwise the same value would be added twice.

## Three ways to measure the same thing

Statisticians can reach GDP from three directions, and in theory all three give the same answer:

1. **Production:** add up the value added at every stage of production.
2. **Expenditure:** add up everything spent on final goods and services.
3. **Income:** add up all the incomes earned, including wages, profits and rents.

## The expenditure formula

The most common version is a simple sum:

| Part | Symbol | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| Consumption | C | Household spending on food, rent, phones, haircuts |
| Investment | I | Businesses buying machines, new homes being built |
| Government | G | Spending on roads, schools, salaries |
| Net exports | X − M | Exports minus imports |

**GDP = C + I + G + (X − M)**

## Real and nominal GDP

Nominal GDP uses current prices, so it rises with inflation even if nothing extra is produced. Real GDP adjusts for price changes, which is why growth rates quoted in the news are almost always real growth.

## What GDP leaves out

GDP is useful, but it was never meant to measure everything.

- **Unpaid work** such as cooking, cleaning and caring for family.
- **Informal activity** that is hard to record.
- **Environmental damage.** Cleaning up a spill adds to GDP; the spill itself subtracts nothing.
- **Fairness.** A rise in average income says nothing about how it is shared.

> The welfare of a nation can scarcely be inferred from a measurement of national income.

Simon Kuznets, who helped design national income accounts, warned about this as early as 1934.

## The takeaway

Treat GDP as a good thermometer for the size of economic activity, not a full health check. Look at it together with jobs, inequality, health and education.
